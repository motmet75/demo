Third-party QR decoder notice
=============================

This app optionally uses @undecaf/zbar-wasm, version 0.11.0, as a local
WebAssembly QR scanner for low-contrast identity-card photos.

ZBar WebAssembly wrapper:
  https://github.com/undecaf/zbar-wasm
  Copyright the contributors as stated in the upstream project.
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL), version 2.1 or later.
  Upstream license: https://github.com/undecaf/zbar-wasm/blob/master/LICENSE

Underlying ZBar barcode reader:
  https://github.com/mchehab/zbar
  License: LGPL-2.1-or-later (refer to upstream license terms).

The application imports the library via NPM and bundles its WebAssembly
locally. The barcode decoder executes in the browser; uploaded QR photos
are not transmitted to third-party scanner services.

ZXing / html5-qrcode remains the initial QR reader. The ZBar component
is activated only as a fallback for difficult image uploads.
